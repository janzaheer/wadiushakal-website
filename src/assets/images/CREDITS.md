## Number plates

We are a UAE operator, so no photo here may show a foreign registration. Where a plate was visible it has
been retouched out (marked "plate removed" below) rather than the photo being swapped — each replacement we
tried simply carried a different foreign plate. Only service-heavy.jpg keeps its plate, because it is a
genuine Abu Dhabi one.

Retouching is a local edit of the plate area only: the surrounding bodywork is interpolated across the gap.
Nothing else in any frame is altered. If you re-crop or replace one of these files, re-check the plate.
